Experiment 2 · Section 1.6

Copy 16 bytes: one powerful instruction vs eighty simple ones

T = N × CPI × Tc. CISC shrinks N. RISC shrinks CPI and Tc. Illustrative timings in the spirit of 1992: a 66 MHz microcoded CISC vs a 200 MHz pipelined RISC.

RISC executes 80× more instructions and still finishes about twice as fast. Intel's answer from the Pentium Pro on: decode x86 into RISC-like micro-ops, so a modern x86 runs like the right-hand machine inside.
