Instrument 2 · Section 2.7.1

The single-cycle core, one phase at a time

Start: x1 = 0x2000, x2 = 7, mem[0x2004] = 0x2A, program at 0x1000. In real hardware all five phases happen inside one clock period; here they are slowed down.

Copper = data path in use this phase; blue = control. The ALU's adder output IEUAdr goes both to the DTIM and back to the PC mux.

0x1000 lw x3, 4(x1)
0x1004 sub x5, x3, x2
0x1008 beq x0, x0, 0x1000
