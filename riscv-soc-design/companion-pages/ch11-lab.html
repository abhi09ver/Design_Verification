Experiment 1 · Sections 11.1.1 and 11.2.1

Two reads to find the page, one to touch the data

Sv32: PTE₁ at satp.PPN × 4096 + VPN[1] × 4 · if pointer, PTE₀ at PTE₁.PPN × 4096 + VPN[0] × 4 · PA = PPN × 4096 + offset, or for a level-1 leaf (4 MiB megapage) PA = PPN[21:10] : VA[21:0]. Physical addresses are 34 bits.

Physical memory: PTEs (address: value, hex)

Edit any PTE; the walk re-runs as you type. A missing address reads as 0 (V = 0).

Experiment 2 · Section 11.2.1.2

Ten flag bits decide pointer, leaf, permission or fault

XWR = 000 pointer · 001 R · 011 RW · 100 X · 101 RX · 111 RWX · 010 / 110 reserved. A pointer must have D = A = U = 0. Under Svade, A = 0 faults any access and D = 0 faults a store.
