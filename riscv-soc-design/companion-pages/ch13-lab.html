Experiment 4 · Section 13.1.5 and Fig. 13.10

Calls push, returns pop; too deep and the oldest addresses are overwritten

A circular stack of N entries. Call at address A pushes A + 4; a return predicts the top and pops. Overflow wraps and overwrites the oldest entry; only the deepest returns then mispredict.
