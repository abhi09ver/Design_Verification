Experiment 2 · Section 20.3, Example 20.3

The highest priority wins, a tie goes to the lowest id, and a claimed source stays silent until it is completed

Requests are levels from the devices. Each clock the gateway sets pending = pending OR (request AND NOT in progress). Claim returns the winner, clears its pending bit and marks it in progress. Complete ends it. MExtInt = (winner's priority > threshold).

Experiment 3 · Section 20.4, Figs. 20.9 to 20.11

Each sample lands in the middle of its bit until the two clocks drift apart by about half a bit over the frame

The transmitter sends one frame. The receiver finds the start edge, waits 8 ticks of its own 16x clock, then samples every 16 ticks. A receiver clock that is fast or slow by e% moves every sample by e% of the time since the edge. Dots show where the receiver samples; a red dot has landed in the wrong bit.
